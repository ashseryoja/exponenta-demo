# Font sources

## Montserrat Armenian
Regular and Bold font files are unmodified files from https://github.com/greghub/montserrat-arm/tree/master/public/fonts .
Armenian adaptation: Vahan Hovhannisyan. Original Montserrat: Julieta Ulanovsky.
The project identifies the fonts as SIL Open Font License: https://github.com/greghub/montserrat-arm/blob/master/index.html .

## Noto Sans Armenian
Self-hosted through @fontsource-variable/noto-sans-armenian.
Source: https://fontsource.org/fonts/noto-sans-armenian
Copyright and full SIL Open Font License are included in Noto-Sans-Armenian-LICENSE.txt.
